03 / BEHIND THE RESEARCH

A computer scientist.
A signal explorer.
A curious human.

I’m Dr.-Ing. Saurav Pahuja, a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Cognitive Systems Lab, University of Bremen, working at the intersection of machine learning, brain signals, and neurotechnology.

My work connects neural decoding with practical systems: from continuous attention tracking and wearable ear-EEG to EEG-guided speech extraction. I’m also exploring how multimodal biosignal AI can learn representations that transfer across people and sensor systems.
